Privacy notice.

What this website collects, and what we do with your details if you contact us.

Who we are

This website is run by AI Technology Futures Ltd (“ATF”), registered in England and Wales, company number 15710953. Registered office: 8 Leinster Square, London, W2 4PL. For anything in this notice, contact hello@aitechfutures.com.

What this website collects

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and has no forms. Fonts and images are served from the site itself, so your browser is not sent to third-party services to load a page.

Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages record standard technical logs, such as IP address, time of request, page requested and browser type. Our hosting provider, Netlify, processes these logs on our behalf to keep the site secure and running.

If you contact us

The buttons on this site open an email to us. If you write, we use your name, email address and the content of your message to reply and to follow up on your enquiry. We do this because it is in our legitimate interest to respond to people who contact us.

We keep correspondence for as long as it is needed to deal with your enquiry and to maintain a record of our business dealings. We do not sell personal information, and we share it only with the service providers that deliver our email and IT systems.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to or restrict how we use it. To do so, email hello@aitechfutures.com.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page if what we collect changes. Last updated: October 2026.
